# Borrower Credit Builder & Funding Strategy Guide v1.0

**Classification:** Borrower-Facing  
**Approved:** September 2026  
**Purpose:** Guide borrowers on business credit building, tiers, vendor reporting, and capital access strategies.

## 1. How Credit Builder Works

The Credit Builder program helps your business establish and build a strong corporate credit profile independent of personal credit:
- **Separating Personal and Business Credit:** Establishing trade lines under your business EIN and D-U-N-S / corporate credit profiles protects personal assets and increases commercial borrowing capacity.
- **Tier-Based Progression:**
  - **Tier 1 (Starter Vendor Accounts):** Net-30 vendor accounts that report prompt payment history to major commercial credit bureaus (Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business, Equifax Business). Starter trade lines help establish your initial business credit scores (such as PAYDEX).
  - **Tier 2 (Store & Fleet Credit):** Retail store and fleet fuel cards that offer higher credit lines and flexible payment terms once Tier 1 accounts have established a track record.
  - **Tier 3 (Revolving Business Credit & Cash Lines):** Unsecured business credit cards and revolving commercial credit lines with major financial institutions.
- **Best Practices:**
  - Always make purchases that meet the vendor's minimum reporting threshold.
  - Pay all invoices well before the due date (net-30 invoices paid within 10-15 days optimize your credit score ratings).
  - Keep accounts active by rotating necessary business supplies through your established trade lines.

## 2. Capital Access Strategy & Roadmaps

- **Pre-Flight Checklist:** Identifies any foundational items required before seeking major capital (e.g., active business registration, good standing with the Secretary of State, commercial address, business phone listing, clean bank statements).
- **Strategy Roadmaps:** A step-by-step sequential action plan tailored to your funding goals, matching your revenue, credit profile, and time-in-business with the most suitable financing products (working capital lines, equipment financing, revenue-based funding, SBA loans).
- **Tracking Progress:** Access the **Capital Access** or **Credit Builder** tab in your portal dashboard to view completed milestones, next recommended actions, and unlocked vendor categories.

## 3. Contacting Support & Next Actions

- For questions regarding specific vendors, report status with Dun & Bradstreet / Experian Business, or funding strategy consultations, consult your account specialist through the portal's support channels.
