# Borrower Portal Navigation & General Support Guide v1.0

**Classification:** Borrower-Facing  
**Approved:** September 2026  
**Purpose:** Guide borrowers on navigating portal features, updating settings, tracking notifications, and communication.

## 1. Navigating Your Borrower Portal

- **Dashboard:** Provides an overview of your active applications, current credit building tier, upcoming task reminders, and recent status updates.
- **Applications:** View your current and past funding applications, continue draft submissions, review term summaries, and upload missing documentation.
- **Credit Builder:** Explore approved tier vendors, view application links for vendor trade lines, and track reported payment accounts.
- **Capital Access / Pre-Flight:** Review readiness checklists and strategic steps recommended for maximizing funding approvals.
- **Settings & Profile:** Update contact information, notification preferences, and manage business profile details.

## 2. Common Questions

- **How long does the application review process take?**  
  Initial pre-qualification reviews typically take 24 to 48 business hours once all required documentation (such as bank statements and owner ID) has been uploaded.
- **How can I upload missing documents?**  
  Navigate to the Documents section or open your active application to upload requested files. PDF format is recommended for statements.
- **Can I speak with a human specialist?**  
  Yes. You can contact your dedicated support team or account specialist via the support contact information provided in your portal header or settings.
