# Borrower Support Knowledge Base & Application FAQ v1.0

**Classification:** Borrower-Facing  
**Approved:** September 2026  
**Purpose:** Official guidance for borrowers navigating their client portal, application statuses, document uploads, and funding roadmap.

## 1. Checking Your Application Status

Borrowers can view real-time updates on their funding applications directly through the Borrower Portal:
- **Navigation:** Navigate to the **Applications** or **My Applications** section from the sidebar menu.
- **Status Indicators:**
  - **Draft / Incomplete:** The application has been started but requires additional details or document uploads before submission.
  - **Submitted / Under Review:** The application package and documents have been successfully received and are currently undergoing underwriting evaluation and pre-qualification analysis.
  - **Information Requested / Documents Needed:** Additional verification documents (such as bank statements, ID, or business verification) are required before processing can continue.
  - **Pre-Qualified / Approved:** The application has met pre-qualification criteria or has received funding offers. Details regarding terms, amounts, and next steps are available in your portal dashboard.
  - **Funded / Completed:** The funding process has finalized and funds have been disbursed according to the agreed terms.

## 2. Document Uploads & Verification Requirements

To ensure fast processing and underwriting accuracy, borrowers should upload clear, unedited, and up-to-date documents:
- **Standard Required Documents:**
  - **Government-Issued Photo ID:** Valid Driver's License or Passport (front and back where applicable).
  - **Business Bank Statements:** Recent 3 to 6 months of complete business bank statements (all pages, in official PDF format).
  - **Proof of Business Ownership / Formation:** Articles of Organization/Incorporation, Operating Agreement, or Business License.
  - **Tax Returns (if requested):** Most recent 1-2 years of business and/or personal tax returns with all schedules.
  - **Voided Business Check:** For verification of disbursement account details.
- **How to Upload Documents:**
  1. Go to the **Documents** or **Upload Center** section in your portal.
  2. Select the document category corresponding to the file being submitted.
  3. Drag and drop or browse to select your PDF/image files.
  4. Ensure all files are legible and click **Upload / Submit**.

## 3. Co-Borrower & Ownership Details

- For businesses with multiple owners holding significant equity (typically 20% or more), co-borrower or guarantor information may be required.
- You can add or update owner and co-borrower details in the **Application Wizard** under the **Ownership Information** step.

## 4. Privacy, Security & Data Protection

- All transmitted information and uploaded documents are encrypted with enterprise-grade security standards.
- Never share sensitive credentials (such as bank passwords, full SSNs, or PINs) in unencrypted chat channels. Upload all sensitive verification documents securely via the dedicated portal upload areas.

## 5. Escalation & Getting Further Help

If you have specific questions regarding a customized offer, pending underwriter review, or need direct human assistance:
- Contact your assigned funding specialist or support team via the **Contact Us** or **Support** link in your portal, or email the support address displayed in your account settings.
