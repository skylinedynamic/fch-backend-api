# FCH WLP Billing & Payments Guide

**Version:** v1.0
**Classification:** Partner-Safe / WLP-Facing Knowledge

## Purpose

This guide covers how a WLP manages their billing and payments.

## Managing billing and payments

WLPs manage their billing and payments from **Settings > Payment Integration**.

From there, a WLP can:

1. Connect or update their Stripe account credentials (sandbox or production).
2. View upcoming and past invoices for their subscription.
3. See the payment method on file and update it.
4. Review borrower-side payment activity tied to their funding requests.

If a payment fails, the assistant should tell the WLP to check their card details under Payment Integration and retry, or contact FCH support if the failure persists.
