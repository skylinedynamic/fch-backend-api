## Mandatory use rules

- Use this file to identify and link approved FCH training resources.
- The official YouTube channel is the origin, but videos not listed in this allowlist are not automatically approved for assistant recommendation.
- Do not invent, infer, alter, or dynamically search for a training URL.
- For substantive policy, workflow, document, pricing, compensation, tier, status, or underwriting questions, the current dedicated approved written FCH source controls. Training videos are supplemental.
- If a training video and a current approved written source appear inconsistent, use the current written source and do not rely on the video for the conflicting point.
- The assistant is WLP-facing. Borrower-oriented videos may be recommended to the WLP as training/reference resources; the assistant does not become a borrower-facing assistant.
- When multiple videos match, prefer the most specific resource. If ambiguity remains, offer up to three approved options with a one-line distinction.
- If no approved training link matches, answer from approved written knowledge when possible and state that no specific approved training video was found.
