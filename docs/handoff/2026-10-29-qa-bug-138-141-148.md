# QA BUG-138, BUG-141, BUG-148

Branch `bugfix/qa-bug-138-141-148` in fch-backend-api and fch-borrower-react, cut from `stage` on 2026-10-29. Fch-admin-frontend-react has no change. Findings: F166, F167, F168 and O73 in `docs/test-findings.md`.

BUG-142 and BUG-143 were fixed and then taken out of this branch on request. Their patches were saved outside the repos.

## BUG-138: Credit Accounts pages repeat and skip cards (backend)

**Problem.** Client portal > Credit Builder > Credit Accounts. QA's 5 pages returned 78-79 rows but only 66-67 distinct cards. About 12 cards were on both page 1 and page 2, and about 12 eligible cards never appeared. The list was ordered by `difficulty_rank` only. Many cards share a rank, and MySQL returns ties in any order, so each page request could order them differently.

**Solution.** `app/Services/Borrower/CreditBuilder/CreditAccountService.php` orders by `difficulty_rank`, then `credit_accounts.id`. No other list orders by the rank alone.

**Tests.** `tests/Feature/CreditBuilder/BorrowerCreditBuilderApiTest::every_offered_card_is_on_exactly_one_page_in_a_fixed_order` checks two things: 20 cards on two pages, each exactly once, and the ORDER BY. SQLite returns ties in insert order, so the repeat itself cannot be reproduced on SQLite.

## BUG-141: White-label logo fallback name unreadable (borrower portal)

**Problem.** When a white-label partner's logo cannot load, the sidebar shows the tenant name instead. It was `#495057` on the navy sidebar.

**Solution.** In fch-borrower-react `assets/css/custom.css`, `.brand-logo-text` uses `--brand-on-sidebar`. That is the readable text colour the branding works out for the sidebar background. See `docs/handoff/2026-10-29-qa-bug-141-logo-fallback-colour.md` in that repo.

## BUG-148: Card logo broken on a Credit Card Stacking step (backend + borrower portal)

**Problem.** On Credit Card Stacking > play > Step 1 (Lowe's Commercial Account) > Manage Status, the card logo did not load, and the browser drew its alt text in a small box.

`image_url` can hold a full logo URL. The admin form takes it as text, and the borrower Credit Accounts page already treats a value starting with `http` as a full URL. But `CreditAccount::full_image_url` always put `storage/` in front, which produced a broken link. The step page also had no fallback for an image that fails to load.

**Solution.**
- Backend: `app/Models/CreditBuilder/CreditAccount.php` `getFullImageUrlAttribute()` returns an `http(s)://` value as it is. An uploaded path keeps `asset('storage/...')`. An empty value gives `null`.
- Borrower portal: new `src/pages/capital-access/components/CardLogo.tsx` is used by `StepDetail.tsx`. It shows the neutral card placeholder when there is no logo or when the logo fails to load, and retries when the URL changes.

**Tests.**
- `tests/Unit/CreditBuilder/CreditAccountImageUrlTest.php` (3 tests)
- borrower `tests/pages/capital-access/CardLogo.test.tsx` (4 tests)

**Left open.** O73: if Lowe's logo on the servers is an uploaded file that is missing, re-upload it. The page now shows the placeholder in the meantime.
